Agent-refined 4D hand–object reconstructions of the real videos

One tab per recording set; the same five objects in each (small metal case, blue tape roll, black spatula, gray mug, dish soap bottle). The FoundationPose + Depth Pro 2 reconstructions after agentic refinement. An automatic gradient-free fit (hand to its keypoints, mask and depth; object to its mask and depth; each finger's flexion with no hand triangle through the object's surface) is followed by Claude (Opus 5.5), which looks at overlays on the video, the 3D scene from any side (hand red where it passes through the object, green where it touches it) and motion plots, labels the motion phases, and fixes what a per-frame fit cannot: contact while the object is held, fingers through it, a still object at rest, jumps. Every edit is a new version, checked before / after. A blind A/B verifier then compares each changed range with the automatic fit (overlays, 3D views from three sides, motion plot) and keeps the fit where the agent's edit loses. Captions: medians before → after. "QC frames" shows, per phase, the initial and refined overlays and the 3D scene from behind and from the left; "report" is the agent's own account of what it changed. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom; the playback bar is at the bottom of each viewer.

The two viewers start from the same camera but play independently: drag each one's time slider.
